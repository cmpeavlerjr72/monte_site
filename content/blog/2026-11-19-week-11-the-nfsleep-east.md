Ladies and gentlemen, I have an unfortunate confession to make. Last Sunday, while anticipating a potentially bad game against the Niners, I walked to my local bar and had a few beers before the game started. As soon as the early window finished up, I walked home and accidentally slept through the entire game. I had the displeasure of waking up as soon as the game ended to see the box score and how everything played out. On one hand, i’m relived I didn’t have to witness that real time, but I did have to watch through the highlights on youtube so I still had to suffer nevertheless.  With that being said, let’s get into the recap.

## Eagles

This team is bad. This team is not fun. This team isn’t healthy. Nobody has been fired.  That’s all we missed over the Eagles’ Bye week. Overall nothing really interesting to say about this team, other than the suffering will continue until morale improves. I don’t expect the head coach to be relieved of his duties unless he severely underperforms for the rest of the season. Eagles have a match against the Steelers this week, and I think the Steelers will win despite the Eagles bye and bad performance they just had.

## Commanders

Hey now, sounds like a called it pretty good last week. Commanders got a huge win against the Giants, and Jayden Daniels continues his streak of never losing to the Giants. Don’t get me wrong, this team is bad and it’s kinda insane they scraped a win out, but a loss here would’ve killed the season so we can’t write them off quite yet! Now they have the Bengals this week, and most of us are predicting blowout, but not me! The NFL has a way of getting interesting down the stretch, which is why i’m predicting the Commanders get the upset here on Monday night!

## Giants

Booooooooo. Giants had the game locked up and should be panicking about this loss. Not because they’re a bad team or anything, but because this was an awful excusable loss. You’re at home, you’ve picked the ball off twice, and you’re on 4th down and 1 to ice the game. Sure maybe they don’t get the yard, but everything leading up to this moment has gone your way and the reward outweighs the risk substantially. Cam Skattebo is an easy 2 yards practically guaranteed. Even worse though was the decision making after. I can understand the risk of not getting the first down, but you’d rather throw 3 bombs towards the end zone than give your kicker a chance to tie the game at home? Even if you don’t trust your kicker, why is he on the roster if not for this exact situation? Just baffling coaching from John Harbaugh. Schedule only gets tougher from here so you better hope you can squeak out a couple wins in games you will largely be the underdog.

## Cowboys

Do I even have to talk about it? We let up a franchise high amount of yards to a defense at home, I think everybody in the world knows our defense sucks. What I refuse to say is that Christian Parker should be fired. I spent all of last season thinking Matt Eberflus was trash and that Micah Parsons had left a larger hole on the defense than anticipated. Well guess what, Eberflus is on the Niners and having them look good, while our defense is still a disaster to look at. Christian Parker is largely going to take all the blame for this, even though it’s on our defense that seemingly has no chemistry with each other. This is why I was crashing out last week when we rushed the field with our entire bench because we got ONE interception. Sure maybe Brian Flores could get in there and make us look unstoppable, but he’s not coming, and we aren’t getting anybody better any time soon. I’m holding our head coach more responsible, because if he was doing his job he would be screaming his lungs out on our team every monday morning. I want our defense scared to show up on the field every day, instead of whatever this cheerleading bull crap we have been watching for the last 11 weeks. Fun fact by the way, Cowboys have yet to win an NFC game not played in the east in two seasons. If we lose to the Titans this week, I want the entire coaching staff fired and Dak to retire since he doesn’t deserve the torture this franchise has put him through these last two years.
