Another week of absolutely disgusting football from this division that would be sad if it wasn't somewhat hilarious. This division may seem depressing, but somehow, it is more competitive than the NFC South and AFC South teams, so I guess I can't complain too much. Following these results, it would seem we are down to two teams that have an actual shot at the playoffs, although the Commanders aren't technically out of it. I simply do not see the Eagle's path to winning the division given the number of talented teams they have left to play, and for that I'm officially writing them off has being dead in the water. I have a lot to complain about this week, so with that being said, let's start with my embarrassing cowboys.

Cowboys (4-5): There is next to nothing to celebrate about this win; yes, we won, yes, it was an away game, and yes, we are still in contention. With those things being said you would think I would be more optimistic about our chances, but that game was so poorly coached from start to finish it's nothing short of a miracle that we were playing a team that had essentially nothing to play for. I could rant about the penalties, but I'd like to mainly address one in particular. We have a team that can barely get one turnover a game and we take a ridiculous unsportsmanlike conduct on a game where we already have double digit penalties. I understand the players celebrations, but there is no reason why Pickens should have rushed the field, and it speaks volumes that our coach doesn't have our players in the right mentality. We have a LOSING record and are SECOND in the division and playing like we don't give a shit. Shotty might not realize it, but he is definitely on the hot seat and needs to stop coaching like a cheerleader if we want to have any chance to actually make the playoffs. It's awesome we have an offense that can drive down in the field one 30 seconds, but we had the ball on the one-yard line with an entire minute left. We should've faked a run and not tried to score instead of giving the ball back with time left, which is just another ridiculous coaching miscue by him. The Colts LET US walk in; I just don't understand why he's missing these easy game time decisions. Now that that's out of the way, I am somewhat optimistic we have a chance against the Niners this weekend. NOT because we are a good football time, but in years past we have looked at this game thinking we had a 50/50 shot to win, but this time we go in expecting to get blown out. Sometimes that magic is all a team needs to get lucky. I still think we probably lose, but I have higher hopes we beat this team than I did the Cardinals, so just don't be too shocked if we pull off the upset.

Eagles (2-7): Boy this is one bad football team. Injuries have decimated this team beyond repair, and there doesn't seem to be any reason to play hard for the rest of the year unless they want to play spoiler maker or help their head coach keep his job. The Eagles fan base from my experience seems to be split 50/50 on whether or not they want this head coach fired. I can understand the argument for; he has underperformed with a healthy roster, he tried to kill Jalen hurts, he uses AI to write half time speeches, but I'm still not entirely sure he deserves the axe. While he may seem bad at hiring offensive coordinators, he has taken them to two Super Bowls and won one of them in recent years. It also doesn't seem helpful that his ownership is shipping of the players that aren't on the IR and leaving him next to nothing to use on the field. While I personally think he is a cornball, I think what he's done in the past should keep him safe this off season, unless the product on the field becomes seriously unwatchable over these next couple weeks. Getting diced up at home by two borderline backup QBs while wearing the Kelly Green certainly didn't help is case.

Giants (5-2-1): Wasn't pretty, but Giants got their first win in Philly in 13 years. Sure, it was against a hobbled Eagles team, but they're pretty banged up as well and could have easily lost this game in a heartbreaker. Isaiah Likely is really good, might even be better than Nabers in terms of being a target threat on the field. JJ took a brutal hit, but he knows he is playing for a starting spot as QB on a team for next year, so he will definitely be playing next week unless Harbaugh forces him to take a game off. It's hard to tell if this team is good enough to win the division since the rest of their schedule is kind of daunting, but it's about as hard as the Cowboys is so maybe they just have to tread water. They'll at least get to beat on the Colts and Eagles one more time, but they will be playing a Commanders team desperate for a win next week, so I'm not so sure they'll be continuing this winning streak much longer. Regardless, it is time to face the harsh truth that this team is finally trending towards being a formidable and frisky team, and they should be set up very nice next season even if they don't win the division this year.

Commanders (2-5-1): Dan Quinn cannot help making the worst possible decisions in the final few minutes of close games. This team is not very good, but they could be so much more in the mix if they would've beaten the Giants in OT a few weeks ago. I just don't understand why you don't draw up a Hail Mary on the final play of the game with a QB who has the most iconic recent Hail Mary play from a few years ago. Sure, you could blame it on Blough and say he is novice coordinator, but with the number of times these terrible decisions keep happening it's got to fall into Dan Quinn's lap at some point. The Commanders have just enough wiggle room left to try and fight for a spot this season, but they aren't going to get much easier games down the stretch and we aren't even really sure if the Rams are good this season given the underwhelming record for their expectations thus far. Even then, its hard to see this team in particular making any noise in the playoffs like they did a couple years ago. The positive is it does seem like Jayden Daniels is capable of being the answer at QB for this team, but I do worry if this is just yet another repeat of RGIII in the making. They will at least be an interesting watch this week against the Giants, but if they do not come away with a win you can kiss this season and Dan Quinn's job goodbye.
