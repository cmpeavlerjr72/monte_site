Hello, Happy Thanksgiving to all of you. I’ve been on the road all day traveling and didn’t have time to put out my most thorough thoughts of the last week, but I wanted to get something out for this week before any of the East plays. Last week I managed to pick every single NFL game right except for the Jags vs Giants, which is ironically the one I was most confident in and needed the most for my Cowboys. Like I said last week, the NFL always finds a way to make the funniest outcomes possible. With that being said, let’s start with the Eagles this week!

Joe Milton beat the Steelers so bad that Mike McCarthy fell asleep during the game! And if you think that’s bad, just wait until he wakes up and sees the Rogers news! Regardless, Eagles have a tough matchup against the Cowboys this week and if Quinyon and Saquan aren’t playing I’m not sure they’ll have enough talent to compete with the Cowboys, even if the defense is abysmal. Season is all but over for them, but they technically are still alive I suppose!

## Cowboys

Dallas absolutely thrashed the Titans, which is exactly what had to happen for everybody to not completely write this team off. I could go on a rant about the offensive statistics and drive performances, but this isn’t exactly breaking news if you’ve watched them play this year. Still impossible to tell if any improvements on defense were actually made, but there is some interesting news that did break the other day. According to sources in the Cowboys, not making the playoffs this year would be seen as a catastrophic let down to the ownership and would largely be blamed on the head coach failing to properly address the defensive coordinator position. Call me crazy, but that loosely translates to “If we miss the playoffs, the coach is fired.” It’s seems pretty safe to say he will coach until the end of the year, but if things get ugly before the Giants matchup who knows. Must win for Cowboys this week.

## Commanders

The Redskins pulled it off! Not gonna brag about saying it was going to happen, mainly because the Commanders made some brain dead coaching decisions towards the end (again) but it’s nice to know this division isn’t a two way race anymore. Redskins have one of the easiest remaining schedules left, and I refuse to believe that the tie with the Giants isn’t going to have some kind of funny implication by the end of the regular season. Commanders play the Cardinals next, and one might think they’ll run away with that game, but truthfully both teams probably think that. If I had to take a guess though, I think the Redskins peel off two more straight. If they do that i’ll have more words on them by then.

## Giants

The funniest outcomes always prevail in the NFL. Not a soul thought they would win this game, and yes there was some Parker Washington shenanigans, but like it or not the Giants won this game. I’m willing to bet they win this game even if it went to overtime, simply because the Giants are so close to having their first good feel season in what feels like forever. The next games are still a gauntlet against 4 current playoff teams, so at this point it’s the Giants division to lose. Will be interesting to see how many of these they can survive with Winston at the helm.
