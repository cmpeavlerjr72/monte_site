Writing on MVPEAV

No Word needed. Log in, tap New article, type, and send it when it's done. Every step below, with a picture.

The short version

  1. Go to mvpeav.com/desk, log in with your username and password (person icon, top right).
  2. Tap New article. Type. It saves itself as you go (the "Saved" line at the top tells you).
  3. H on the toolbar turns the line you're on into a section heading (Eagles, Cowboys…). B and I are bold and italic. Paste from anywhere, it comes in clean.
  4. Insert puts something between two paragraphs: a request for me (a stat, a picture, a clip, a tweet), a link (YouTube, X, Getty, anything), or a photo.
  5. Lookup is for when you need a fact while you're writing: ask, keep typing, the answer shows up in a minute or two with its sources.
  6. Send to the editor when it's done. It goes to my queue; I fix typos, add the pictures and clips, and publish. Your words stay yours.

Sending it in

Your X handle

The first time you log in, the desk asks for your X handle (you can say Not now). Save it and two things happen on their own: when one of your articles goes out, the site posts it on X and tags you, and your byline on the article links readers to your account. You can add or change it any time under Your login at the bottom of the desk.

Two ways to send a piece

When you tap Send to the editor you pick what should happen to it:

  • It's finished: use my words as written. The way it has always worked. Your words go up exactly as you wrote them; the editor fixes typos, adds pictures and clips, and publishes.
  • It's an outline: have BlogBot write it. New. Type an outline instead of an article: a heading (use H) for each section, bullet points under each for what that section should say, your opinions included. Send it, and BlogBot researches it, builds the piece around your headings in your order, and writes a full draft under your name. It takes a few minutes. Back on the desk, the article shows BlogBot is writing it, then an Edit it button appears.

Editing what BlogBot wrote. Tap Edit it and you get the same editing page MVPeav uses. Tap any paragraph to rewrite it in your own words, add your color, cut what you don't like. Tap a picture to swap, move or remove it, or use + between paragraphs to add a photo, a clip, a table or a graphic. The Draft menu has Rewrite all with a note if you want the bot to take another pass ("more about the defense", "less stats"). The page may also ask you a question or two the outline raised, like your own line on a game you were at; answer them and the bot works your answer in. When you're happy, you're done: the editor publishes it. Your X handle rides along, so the post tags you.

The image bot

Finding pictures is the slow part. When you send a piece, tick Let the image bot fill in pictures and every section gets at least one picture idea or a graphic made for it: a real game photo where one fits what the section talks about, and a branded graphic (logos, team colors, the numbers from the section) where no photo does. The ideas land in the editor's tray to approve, and the graphics drop into the piece by themselves. On the editing page the same thing is in the Draft menu as Image bot, so you can run it after you've made changes too.

Good to know

  • Your articles in progress are listed on the desk. Open one with Keep writing; it picks up where you left off, on any device.
  • If the connection drops, keep typing: the page keeps a copy on your device and saves when it's back.
  • After you send one, it's read-only. Make a copy if you want to work from it again.
  • Still have a Word file? The drop box is under "Have a Word file or text to paste instead?" on the desk.
